# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want QR Dines to be a respectful, inclusive, and productive environment for everyone who participates.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussions
- Assume good intent and focus on the problem, not the person
- Welcome different viewpoints and experiences
- Be mindful of how your words and actions affect others

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling
- Publishing someone’s private information without consent
- Threats of violence or encouraging self-harm

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:

- For **security-related** concerns, follow `SECURITY.md`.
- For conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers using GitHub (for example via a private message if available, or by opening an issue if you can’t reach a maintainer privately).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban users from participation for behavior that violates this code of conduct.
